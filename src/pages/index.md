<p class="tagline">Backend engineer. I like understanding how systems work from the ground up.</p>

Hi, I’m Varun. I build backend systems, and the parts I care about most are the ones nobody sees: what happens between a
request arriving and a response leaving, and what breaks when scale or failure shows up. I’m drawn to distributed systems
and to taking things apart until I know why they behave the way they do.

Today I work on [Smartsheet](https://smartsheet.com)’s authentication platform, which is new territory for me and a fun 
one to take apart. Before that I built developer tooling at [Hasura](https://hasura.io) and consumer-scale logistics at [Blinkit](https://blinkit.com), and I started
my career on the frontend at [Norton](https://norton.com).

[//]: # (so I’m learning it the slow way: reading the specs, breaking things, and writing down what I find.)

That’s what this blog is for: working things out and writing down what I learn.
